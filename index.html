Every customer begins as a transaction. A sign-up, a first deposit, a support ticket.

Nobody stays for a transaction. They stay because someone on the other side knows their name, remembers their last problem and picks up before they have to ask twice.

That's the job I've done everywhere, from school quizzes to trade fairs to crypto sports trading: turn the transaction into a relationship. At DG3 that meant nearly doubling new-user activation, looking after the five whales behind most of the sportsbook's volume, and winning back the biggest one when he went quiet.

Five volumes below. Take your time. 縁

Able Jobs (YC)

Able Jobs, a YC-backed startup, helps companies hire early-career talent. As an operations intern I worked the whole match, from database to joining day, for partner companies including Verzeo, Pitstop and Byju's.

The job wasn't done at "you're hired". It was done when they showed up.

CH. 3Activation · From Mar 2026

In March 2026 I took over new-user activation and found a leak. Our onboarding journey only caught people in their first session, so anyone who visited, left and came back to sign up was never tagged as new. 4 in 10 new users were quietly missing onboarding.

We built a custom user attribute that keys the journey on where a user is, not how many sessions they've had, and I rebuilt the lifecycle email journeys around it. Signup-to-active went from 31% to 59% in two months.

CH. 4DG3 Terminal launch · 2026

One of our biggest traders hadn't placed a single trade since the terminal went live. What brought him back wasn't a campaign or an offer. He was going through something serious, and I moved the conversation to a private chat, listened, and stayed in his corner until he got proper answers. A few weeks later he was trading again: $2M+ on the terminal in under a month.

Alongside the launch I built DG3 Esports on X from zero, built Esports Signals (see Inside My Head), and built the waitlist signup flow myself: a Supabase* webhook sends an HTML email through Resend*, confirming 550+ signups.
